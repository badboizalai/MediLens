# Data Sharing Agreement — outline (hospital partner → MediLens)

1. Parties, contacts, DPOs
2. Definitions (personal data, de-identified data, derived models)
3. Purpose and permitted uses; prohibited uses (re-identification, resale)
4. Legal basis on the partner side; ethics committee approval reference and conditions
5. Data description, volume, format, de-identification standard and verification sample
6. Transfer method and location of processing (OTIA if abroad)
7. Security measures (reference MediLens Security Evidence Pack)
8. Access list and training of personnel
9. Retention period, return/deletion and certificate of deletion
10. Breach notification between parties (timeline aligned with the 72-hour authority notice)
11. Publications and attribution
12. Intellectual property: labels, models, results
13. Audit and reporting
14. Term, termination, survival clauses
15. Governing law and dispute resolution
