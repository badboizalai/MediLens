# Intended Use Statement — MediLens <version>

**Intended purpose:** MediLens helps <users> review and understand information that is already written in their own health records, by showing findings on illustrative body models and providing sourced educational explanations.

**Intended users:** adults managing their own records; caregivers managing a dependant's profile; clinicians viewing a record shared with them (view only).

**Inputs:** photos/PDFs of records; digital records (text PDF, DOCX, FHIR); free-text descriptions labelled as user-reported; (v2) CT series used only to build illustrative anatomy.

**Outputs:** structured findings confirmed by the user; 2D/3D illustrations; timeline; educational explanations with citations.

**MediLens does NOT:** diagnose; detect, measure or characterise disease on images; recommend or change treatment or medication; predict outcomes or survival; triage urgency (except always directing emergencies to 115); replace a clinician.

**Use environment:** web browser on personal devices; not for emergency use.
