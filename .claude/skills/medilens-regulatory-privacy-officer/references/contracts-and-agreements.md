# Contracts and agreements — minimum content

## Terms of use (users)
Intended use and limits; not medical advice; user responsibilities (own data, accuracy); acceptable use; sharing rules; liability limits within the law; changes and re-consent; governing law; contact.

## Privacy notice
See pdpl-obligations.md "Notice" row.

## Data sharing agreement (hospital partner)
Parties and roles; purpose (research/model improvement), data description and de-identification standard; legal basis and ethics approval reference; security measures; no re-identification; location of processing (and OTIA); retention and deletion; publication rules; audit rights; breach notice; termination; IP of derived models. Outline in `assets/data-sharing-agreement-outline.md`.

## Annotator agreement (medical students)
Task scope; data handled (synthetic/de-identified only); NDA; assignment of rights in labels; pay rate and schedule; quality expectations; no copies kept; equipment and security rules; termination.

## Vendor / processor (AWS, LLM, OCR, email)
DPA; processing only on instructions; no training on customer data; region; sub-processors; security certifications; breach notice timeline; deletion on exit; audit information.

## Pentest engagement
Scope, rules of engagement, time window, test accounts with synthetic data, data handling of findings, disclosure, liability.

## Content licences
Every dataset/asset: licence name and URL, training right, product-use right, commercial use, share-alike obligations, attribution text — recorded in the License Register (data curator skill).
