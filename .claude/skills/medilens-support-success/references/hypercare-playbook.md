# Hypercare playbook (4 weeks after each major release)

Daily: triage all new tickets by 10:00; review error/latency dashboards with SRE; safety-flag review with CSO; publish the daily report; fast-track S1/S2 fixes (hot-fix branch, PB-05 light).
Weekly: themes to PM; KB updates; release of accumulated fixes.
Exit criteria (gate G5-H): no new S1/S2 for 14 consecutive days · SLOs met for 14 days · open S3 backlog not growing week over week · no open safety class A/B items · KB covers the top-10 questions. Decision recorded by Support + SRE, approved by QC.
