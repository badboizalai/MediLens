# Knowledge base guide
- Title = the user's question in their words ("Tại sao ảnh của tôi bị từ chối?").
- Answer in the first two sentences; then steps; then "still stuck?" with the contact path.
- Screenshots with synthetic data only; alt text for every image.
- VI and EN versions via the technical writer; medical topics reviewed by the CSO.
- Each article: owner, version, review date, related tickets count; retire articles with no views for 2 quarters.
