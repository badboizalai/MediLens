# Success metrics
| Metric | Definition | Target (v1) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First response time (FRT) | created → first human reply, business hours | p90 within SLA |
| Resolution time | created → resolved | p90 within SLA by severity |
| CSAT | 1–5 survey after resolution | ≥ 4.3 |
| Ticket rate | tickets per 100 active users per week | falling after hypercare |
| Self-service rate | KB views that end without a ticket | rising |
| Safety-flag rate | safety tickets per 1,000 documents | tracked; every one reviewed |
| Activation | users who confirm ≥ 1 document within 7 days | from product dashboard |
| Retention | users active in week 4 | from product dashboard |
