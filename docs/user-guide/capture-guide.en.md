# How to photograph your medical record

A clear photo helps MediLens read your record correctly. It takes about a minute.

## Before you start
- Use a bright place with daylight or an even room light.
- Put the page flat on a plain table. Smooth out folds.

## Take the photo
1. **One page per photo.** Take each page separately.
2. **Whole page in view.** All four corners and edges must be visible.
3. **Hold the phone straight above** the page, not at an angle.
4. **Avoid glare and shadows.** Tilt the page or move away from lamps and windows. Do not use the flash on glossy paper.
5. **Keep still and tap to focus.** The text should look sharp when you zoom in.
6. **Move closer** until the page fills the frame, but do not cut off any edge.

## Protect your privacy
- Cover other people's names or details (for example, another patient on a shared sheet) before you take the photo.
- Take photos in a private place.
- Only upload records you are allowed to share.

## What is accepted
JPG, PNG, HEIC or PDF, up to 20 MB per file and 30 pages per document.

## If we ask you to retake
We may say the photo is blurry, too dark or too small. Follow the tip shown, then try again. This is normal and does not mean you did anything wrong. If you upload the same file twice, we will warn you and will not create a second record.

## What happens next
1. MediLens reads the photo. You can see the progress and leave the page.
2. You get a **draft**. Please check each field, especially those marked yellow.
3. After you confirm, the information appears on the body view.

MediLens helps you understand your record. It does not give a diagnosis or treatment advice. Please ask your doctor about your health. <!-- claims-ok -->
